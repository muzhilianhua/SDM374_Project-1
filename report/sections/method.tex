\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
The policy sees the 2,048 training rows and the results of its own calls, nothing else. It keeps the incumbent
$W^\ast$ (the best point measured so far; initially the best training row, which is never resent) and proposes 50 new
points per call.

\subsection{Additive Block Descent}
\label{sec:block}
Let $B_t$ be the \emph{base} of call $t$ and $B_t[b\!\leftarrow\!w]$ the base with block $b$ replaced by $w$. Every
candidate except the combinations below has this single-block form, so its measured effect
$\delta_b(w)=R(B_t[b\!\leftarrow\!w])-R(B_t)$ belongs to one building. Each building keeps a table $v_b$ of measured
blocks and their effects relative to the incumbent. Under additivity,
\begin{equation}
\hat R(w_1,w_2,w_3)=R(W^\ast)+\textstyle\sum_{b=1}^{3}\bigl(v_b(w_b)-v_b(w^\ast_b)\bigr),
\label{eq:add}
\end{equation}
which needs no model fit. The base of the next call is the combination with the lowest $\hat R$ among the incumbent's
block and the two best improving blocks of each building; up to six further combinations are sent with it. The
design gives three properties at once: every point is an attributable experiment, one batch advances three
7-dimensional searches in parallel, and improvements found in different buildings are composed at the cost of one
point each. It is a block-coordinate descent~\cite{bcd} that uses additivity as additive Bayesian optimisation
does~\cite{addgp}, but with measured rather than modelled block effects.

Within a block, candidates come from four \emph{move classes}: \emph{surrogate} picks, the lowest lower confidence bound
(mean $-$ std) among 1,500 perturbations, from a ridge trend plus an ARD GP on the 600 rows nearest the base;
\emph{random} moves at a per-building trust scale that grows by 1.5 after a success and shrinks by 0.6 after a failure,
within $[0.01,1]$~\cite{turbo}; \emph{fine} moves (small multiplicative steps, zeroing a minor weight, or a tiny share
between the 0.1\,\% and 10\,\% quantiles of the training weights, $1.6\times10^{-6}$ to $7.2\times10^{-3}$); and, from
call 9, \emph{scans}, one-coordinate steps just outside the known plateau plus the previous call's improving
coordinates re-applied to the new base. In calls 1--6, shift moves favour objectives whose share correlates
negatively with $R$ in the training rows (variant v2).

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{One call of the policy (v4)}
\label{alg:call}
\small
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require training rows, this run's measured points, seed, call index $t$
\State $W^\ast\gets$ best measured point; update block tables $v_b$, trust scales, null intervals, hit counts $h_c,n_c$
\State $B_t\gets\arg\min\hat R$ over combinations of each building's two best blocks~\eqref{eq:add}
\State batch $\gets\{B_t\}\,\cup$ up to six next-best combinations
\State compute class shares $s_c$ by~\eqref{eq:share}
\For{building $b=1,2,3$ and class $c$}
\State draw $s_c n_b$ blocks $w$ of class $c$ from $B_t[b]$ ($n_b$: free slots of $b$, rounded), skipping null intervals and sent points
\State batch $\gets$ batch $\cup\,\{B_t[b\!\leftarrow\!w]\}$
\EndFor
\State send the 50 points; record every $R$ \Comment{randomness seeded by (seed, $t$)}
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{Evidence-Driven Allocation}
\label{sec:alloc}
Fixed class shares spend most slots where little is found: with shares 50/25/25 the surrogate held 44\,\% of the
points but hit only 3.5\,\% of the time. Variant v4 adds three rules that use the run's own results only.
\emph{Plateau filter:} a one-coordinate move that returns exactly $R(B_t)$ ($|\delta|\le10^{-9}$) marks a null interval
for that building, base block and coordinate; no later move is proposed inside the widest such interval.
\emph{Posterior shares:} with $h_c$ hits (points below their call's base) among $n_c$ points of class $c$ over the last
four calls,
\begin{equation}
s_c=f_c+\Bigl(1-\textstyle\sum_{c'}f_{c'}\Bigr)\frac{r_c}{\sum_{c'}r_{c'}},\qquad r_c=\frac{h_c+1}{n_c+5},
\label{eq:share}
\end{equation}
a Beta(1,4) posterior mean with floors $f_c=0.25$ for surrogate picks and 0.10 otherwise, so no class is starved.
\emph{No repeats:} a candidate equal to a training row or a sent point is never proposed, so every call sends 50 new
points.

\subsection{Deployment}
\label{sec:deploy}
The submitted policy (h7) is v4 for calls 1--11; from call 12 it replaces up to 12 fine or zeroing slots by coarser
single-coordinate, multi-coordinate and restart moves. All randomness derives from (seed, call), and the graded
package pins the BLAS kernel and thread count, because the GP hyperparameter fit amplifies rounding differences into
different selections. The package writes each batch to disk before sending it, never resends a charged call, and
can recompute every batch from its log without the simulator.
